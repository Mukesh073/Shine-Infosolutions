Q1. Why choose custom software over ready-made (SaaS) products?

Ready-made software forces you to change your business process to fit its limitations. Custom software development involves building an application designed specifically for your unique operational workflow.

  • Efficiency: You only get features you actually need, eliminating clutter and improving workflow speed.
  • Scalability: The software grows with your business. You aren't limited by a third-party vendor's update cycle.
  • Integration: We create seamless connections to your existing tools (like Tally or CRM) for a unified data flow.
  • Long-Term ROI: You avoid recurring monthly subscription fees and own the intellectual property, turning a cost into a valuable business asset.

Q2. How much does custom software cost?

The cost of custom software varies significantly based on project complexity. The primary factors include:

  • The total number and complexity of features required.
  • The intricacy of the UI/UX design.
  • The need for integration with other third-party systems.
  • The underlying technology stack chosen for scalability and security.

After a detailed requirement analysis, we provide a transparent proposal. As a leading provider of custom software solutions in Gorakhpur, we focus on delivering value without hidden costs.

Q3. How long does the development process take?

Timelines are directly related to project scope. A simple application with core features might take 4-8 weeks. A medium-sized business management tool often takes 3-6 months. Q11. How do you handle data migration from my old software or Excel sheets?

Data migration is a critical step in replacing a legacy system. Our process ensures data integrity and minimal disruption to your operations. We follow a standard ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) procedure:

  1. Extract: We carefully extract all existing data from your current database, Excel files, or old software.
  2. Transform: Data is often messy or formatted inconsistently. We clean, validate, and re-structure the data to fit the new application's database schema. This step prevents errors in the new system.
  3. Load: After thorough testing and verification, we load the cleaned data into the new application during the final deployment phase (cutover), usually scheduled during off-peak hours.

A successful data migration strategy is a core component of our custom software development services.

A strong partnership between your team and our custom software development company in Gorakhpur ensures the final product truly meets business expectations.

Q13. What does "scalability" actually mean for my business growth?

Scalability is the ability of the software to grow with your business without breaking down or requiring a complete rebuild. We design for scalability in two ways:

  • Performance Scalability (Vertical/Horizontal Scaling): The software architecture must handle an increasing number of users, transactions, or data volume. This means designing efficient code and databases that can be easily upgraded with more server resources (CPU, RAM) as user load increases.
  • Feature Scalability (Modularity): The application is built in modules. This allows you to add entirely new functionalities in the future (e.g., adding an inventory module to an existing billing software) without affecting current operations. This modular approach provided by a skilled IT company in Gorakhpur protects your initial investment.

Q14. What is the difference between post-launch warranty and an AMC?

It's important to differentiate between fixing errors and adding new features. We offer both, but they serve different purposes:

  • Warranty Period (Bug Fixing): Included free for a specified period after launch (e.g., 60 days). The warranty covers fixing any bugs or errors where the software does not behave exactly as specified in the original project agreement.
  • Annual Maintenance Contract - AMC (Proactive Support): This is an optional, ongoing service contract for long-term health. An AMC typically includes proactive monitoring, security updates, minor tweaks, performance optimization, and priority technical support. New feature development or major changes are usually billed separately as new project phases.
